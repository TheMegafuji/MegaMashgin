CREDITS & SCOPE

Open art. Original details.

Twenty food models come from Kenney Food Kit 2.0, licensed CC0. They were rendered into static images for this project. Drink variations, packaging, the concept mark, layouts and synthesized sounds are original code-based work. Interface icons come from Lucide, under the ISC license.

This is an independent portfolio/take-home project, not an official Mashgin product or store. The visual direction references Mashgin's public website; the concept mark is an original interpretation. Payments, product names and prices are fictional. This demo does not implement computer vision or fulfill food orders.
